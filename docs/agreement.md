## Guiding values and principles
*   Teamwork: Everyone contributes to the success of the team.
*   Respect: For each other, the instructors, and the course.
*   Accountability: Everyone attending meetings and doing their assigned tasks.
*   Mutual support: Using our strengths to help one another learn and solve problems.

## Operating rhythm

### Meeting schedule
*   Frequency: Weekly on Mondays
*   Time: 9PM EST / 6PM PST
*   Platform: Zoom / Slack

### Communication rules
*   Standard response delay: Maximum 24 hours.
*   Deadline response delay: Maximum 12 hours (within 3 days of a deliverable).
*   Methods: Slack (Primary), Phone/Text (Backup).

### Work commitment
*   Average: 5 hours per week per member.
*   Note: Hours may increase leading up to major deliverable deadlines.

## Conflict resolution
If a conflict arises from non-adherence to agreed responsibilities:
1.  Open discussion: Immediately hold a meeting with the entire team to explain problematic behaviors and suggest resolutions.
2.  Perspective sharing: Invite the individual to explain their perspective and discuss a resolution plan as a group.
3.  Instructor intervention: If the issue cannot be resolved internally, contact the instructor for moderation.
